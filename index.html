How it works

From a file to every platform in four steps

Castora Studio sends links, not files. So first turn your image or video into a Direct link.

1

Turn media into a link

Upload your image or video to a free host (details below) and copy the direct link.

2

Add your title

Type a title or idea, such as "48 Laws of Power" and Paste your direct link.

3

Pick platforms

Choose where it should go. Each platform gets its own caption style and length.

4

Cast

Castora Studio sends your content to an automated workflow which publishes it across your social platforms.

Making a link that never expires

Postimages Images

  1. Open postimages.org and choose your image.
  2. Set the expiration to No expiration.
  3. Upload, then copy the Direct link (it ends in .jpg or .png).
  4. Paste it into the Image link box.

Catbox Images & videos

  1. Open catbox.moe and pick your file (leave the temporary option off).
  2. Click upload and copy the link it gives you.
  3. It looks like files.catbox.moe/xxxxxx.mp4.
  4. Paste it into the Video link box.

Quick check: paste your link in a new browser tab. If the image or video opens by itself with no page around it, it is a direct link and will work. If you see a web page instead, you copied the wrong link.
